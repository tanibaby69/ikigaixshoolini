How to Win

To be eligible, your team must submit the following. Focus on impact, technical skill, and a clear presentation.

Your Deliverables

🚀
01

Working Prototype / Demo

Submit a working build, hosted link, or a clear video demonstration.

📊
02

Pitch Deck (Max 7 Slides)

Clearly explain the problem, your solution, tech stack, and impact.

🎬
03

3-Minute Video (Optional)

Highly recommended. This is the fastest way for judges to get it.

Judging Criteria

50%

Prototype & Impact

How functional, innovative, and impactful is the final solution?

30%

Technical Implementation

How well is the tech stack, architecture, and logic executed?

20%

Pitch & Presentation

Clarity, storytelling, and the ability to communicate your idea.
